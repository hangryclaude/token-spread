# Sweep 14 — take from GitHub

**274 entries · 59 pass · 70 on the provider's word · 89 rejected · 56 unresolved.**

Forty-eight new entries, ids 226–273. Twelve pass.

Every earlier cohort started from provider documentation and worked outward. This one started
from code: six miners over six GitHub territories — agent harnesses, the MCP ecosystem, the
2025-26 self-host serving wave, official-SDK issue trackers, batch-pipeline tooling, and
billing-audit OSS — each candidate cited to a file path or issue and quoted verbatim. Then the
register's usual discipline: a dedup pass against all 226 existing entries (5 duplicates
dropped, 53 mined → 48 kept), adjudication with mandatory re-fetch of every cited source, and
**two independent refuters per passing verdict** — one on evidence, one on mechanism.

The refuters did what they did to sweep 13's passes:

| | |
|---|---:|
| candidates mined | 53 |
| duplicates of existing entries | 5 |
| adjudicated | 48 |
| verdicts lowered under challenge | **21** |
| of the 32 initially adjudicated passing, surviving as passes | **12** |

The commonest downgrade was PASS_ABSOLUTE → CONTRACTUAL_ONLY (six times): an adjudicator
accepts a provider's or a repo's own sentence about what its mechanism does, and the evidence
lens points out that a sentence is still just a sentence. Five more claimed passes fell all the
way to FAIL on the mechanism lens.

One process note, owed to sweep 13: that sweep's challenge ran in both directions — refuters at
the passes, an advocate at the rejections. This sweep ran refuters only. Its 20 FAILs and 7
unresolveds have not had an advocate argue them up, so if this cohort carries a too-harsh
verdict, that check is still owed.

Where the twelve passes cluster is itself the finding: **eight are accounting corrections** —
cost dashboards (Aider's display, Helicone's calculator, Langfuse three separate ways,
tokencost's dead price-sync) mis-reading bills by 2.2x to 163x in both directions. Nothing on
the wire changes; the bill was simply wrong, or wrongly read. The other four are self-host
placement mechanics (KV-aware routing, prefill-disaggregation bypass) and one byte-identical
partial-batch resubmission. The pattern matches the register's oldest lesson: the savings that
survive the bar are mostly not techniques at all — they are corrections to instruments.

`↻` marks an entry whose verdict changed under challenge. Twenty-one of forty-eight did.
