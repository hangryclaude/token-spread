One maintainer resolution is recorded inside its entry rather than applied silently: a
mechanism refuter lowered Fireworks' default-on prompt cache to FAIL on the batch-nondeterminism
argument — a cache hit can land a request in a different batch shape, so byte-identical output
is a stronger claim than a docs sentence can carry. The argument is real, and it indicts the
entire vendor-asserted-identity class equally, which is exactly why it cannot fail one vendor
alone: the class caps at CONTRACTUAL_ONLY, and the tension is preserved in the entry's trap
field where every future reader of that verdict class will meet it.

Where the three passes land: instrument corrections and structural observations, as ever —
OpenCost's per-token GPU cost allocation reads billing exhaust without touching a request,
Hugging Face's scale-to-zero idles the meter by sending nothing, and Claude Code's SessionStart
resume fields expose cache-lineage cost metadata the transcript already carries. The pattern
the register keeps re-teaching held on every new surface: what survives adjudication is almost
never a lever on the model — it is a correction to the instruments watching the bill.
