The pool

Prepaid seats. Metered honestly. Nobody owes anything.

A private pool of Claude API seats for people who use a little: pay in what you like, spend it at Anthropic's own list rates with zero markup, and get switched off — never billed — the moment your credit runs out.

The whole deal

Four steps, one of which is Venmo.

There is no checkout, no subscription, no card on file. Money moves between people; the ledger only ever records it.

venmo→credit recorded→ your own API key→metered until it's spent

Your seat is your own Anthropic workspace and your own key — no shared logins, nothing against the rules. Claude Code picks the key up from one environment variable and works exactly as it always does.

What a $30 seat looks like

Pay in any amount. Watch it, or don't — the meter watches for you.

Every minute of usage is priced at Anthropic's published rates and subtracted from your balance. You get told before it matters, and stopped before it costs anyone.

The held-back sliver

Anthropic's usage meter runs about five minutes behind reality. A small slice of your balance — your own peak burn rate times that blind window — is held back so the pause always lands before zero. It's still your money; it just can't be promised twice.

Paused means paused: top up and it resumes. Debt is not a state this system has.

Who it's for

Light users only — and we check, with a measurement.

Before a seat exists, the free audit runs on your machine and prices your last thirty days of Claude usage at list rates. Under half the seat price: you're in, and the pool genuinely saves you money versus a flat subscription.

$0.00markup, by design — metered cost is the whole price. Charging more would be resale, which Anthropic's terms prohibit.
$8,100what one heavy Claude Code user metered in 26 days. If your audit reads like that, a flat subscription is your deal — the pool will tell you so and point you there.
100%of unused credit is refundable when you leave. It was always your money.

The pool is 3–5 people who know each other, prepaid, run at cost by one operator who carries the liability. It is deliberately not a product for strangers.

The receipts

Two meters, and they check each other nightly.

Your balance page is generated from an append-only ledger that prices every request itself — and every night that ledger is compared against Anthropic's own cost report. If they ever disagree by more than a cent and a rounding hair, everything freezes until a human explains why.

What you see

A one-page balance sheet: paid in, used, held back, spendable, status. No dashboards, no login — it's a file, sent to you, that opens anywhere.

What you never see

Your prompts. The meter reads token counts and dollar amounts from Anthropic's usage API — the content of what you ask Claude never passes through the pool at all.
